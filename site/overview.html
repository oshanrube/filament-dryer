Filament Dryer

3D-printed filament dryer boxes for a fleet of Creality printers: one box per printer, printed in ABS/ASA, heated to 40-70 °C, weighing the reel and reporting to the Printer Control Center over Wi-Fi. Two versions share the same heater, fans, controller, sensors and 12 V feed, so they use the same spares.

Plan as of 2026-11-23. Pick a version from the tabs above.

2-roll feeder

Two reels and a feeder pod. When the printer reports a runout, the box feeds the second reel in and the print resumes on its own.

One reel, no feeders. Smaller and simpler; when the reel runs out the printer pauses as it does today.

1 roll v2 (twin scale)

Controller board (JLCPCB assembly)

One controller board serves both versions: the 2-roll build fits everything, the 1-roll build leaves the feeder parts off. It carries an ESP32-C3-WROOM-02 module with a USB-C port, the 5 V and 3.3 V supplies, the MCP23017, the HX711 load-cell amplifiers, the TMC2209 feeder drivers, a 12 V gate driver and MOSFET for the heater, the fan and buzzer switches, and JST XH connectors numbered as in the wiring diagrams. Every part is in JLCPCB's library, so the board arrives fully assembled; the parts cost about US$16 per 2-roll board and US$9 per 1-roll board, plus JLCPCB's fees. Every LCSC part number was checked on JLCPCB on 2026-11-24.
