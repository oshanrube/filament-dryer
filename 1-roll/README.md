# 1 roll

One reel on a load cell, no feeders. The shared JLCPCB-assembled controller board without the feeder parts, a BTT Smart Filament Sensor V2 at the exit, 12 V dual-ball-bearing fans, the same 12 V 150 W heater and 12 V feed as the 2-roll box.

- `page.html`: page content (built into `docs/1-roll/` by `tools/build_site.py`)
- `bom.csv`: parts list
- The electronics are on the shared controller board: schematic in `pcb/schematic.svg`, JLCPCB BOM in `pcb/jlcpcb/`
- `images/`: from `tools/gen_drawings.py`
