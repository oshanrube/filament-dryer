# Two-reel filament dryer

A 3D-printed, two-reel filament dryer and dispenser, one per printer. Both reels sit on bearing rollers in a heated chamber (40-70 °C, 12 V 150 W PTC heater); each reel is weighed on its own load cell. When the printer reports a filament runout, the box feeds the second reel in and the print resumes on its own. An ESP32-C3 SuperMini running ESPHome reports both reels, temperature and humidity to the Printer Control Center over Wi-Fi.

## Key decisions

- One box per printer, two reels, automatic switch-over on the printer's runout report.
- 12 V feed from a shared HP 1600 W server supply over XT60; no mains in the box.
- Heated chamber on top, insulated floor, cool electronics bay below; feeders in a side pod outside the heat.
- Housing in ABS or ASA only.
